Turn 1  ·  Identity

Prereq — four logo directions

All four are monoline, geometric, and built to survive at 16px. Each takes a different position on the core belief — that a market price is not the same thing as the truth. Reference them in chat as 1a, 1b, 1c, 1d.

1A The Prefix
prereq.
A P built from one stem and one bowl, with a full stop set out on its own baseline — the thing that comes before the sentence. Warmest and most brandable of the four; reads as software, not as a chart.

prereq
